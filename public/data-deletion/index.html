Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page describes how to request deletion of personal information held by Journey Expert Limited in connection with JEL Angela Customer Assistant, our websites, Facebook Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp customer support.

How to make a request

  1. Email journeyexpertbd@gmail.com with the subject JEL Angela Data Deletion Request.
  2. Provide the name and email address or phone number you used with us, and the relevant service or channel (website, Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp). Include an approximate interaction date if known.
  3. Specify the information you want deleted. Do not include passwords, login tokens, OTPs, passport numbers or payment details.

How we handle deletion requests

We review requests and may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify identity and locate records. Where applicable, we delete or de-identify data under our control, except where retention is necessary for legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution obligations. We will explain any applicable limitations when responding.

Removing Meta access

To revoke future access, review the connected applications in your Facebook or Meta account settings and remove JEL Angela Customer Assistant if listed. This may not delete records previously stored with Journey Expert Limited; use the email procedure above for those records. Removing the app does not delete data held separately by Meta.
