This page explains how to request deletion of personal information that Journey Expert Limited holds in connection with JEL Angela Customer Assistant, our websites and customer support through Facebook Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp.

How to request deletion

  1. Email journeyexpertbd@gmail.com with the subject JEL Angela Data Deletion Request.
  2. Provide the name and email address or phone number you used when contacting us, the relevant service or channel (website, Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp), and an approximate interaction date if you know it.
  3. Describe the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, access tokens, one-time passwords, passport numbers or payment details.

What happens next

We will review your request and may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. Where applicable, we will delete or de-identify personal information within our control. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution obligations. We will explain any applicable limitation when responding to your request.

Disconnect access via Meta

You can review connected applications in your Facebook or Meta account settings and remove JEL Angela Customer Assistant if it appears there. Disconnecting app access may prevent future access, but it does not necessarily delete information previously stored in our systems. To request deletion of stored records, use the email instructions above. Removing access to this app does not itself remove information Meta holds separately.
