Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Refunds and cancellations depend on the confirmed service and applicable third-party supplier rules.

General rule

Refund and cancellation rights depend on the specific service, airline, hotel, tour operator, institution, payment provider or other supplier involved. Journey Expert will apply the confirmed supplier terms to the relevant transaction.

Air tickets

Ticket cancellation, reissue and refund depend on fare rules, airline penalties, no-show status, taxes and supplier processing. Some fares may be non-refundable.

Hotels, tours and packages

Cancellation windows, penalties and refundable amounts depend on the confirmed booking terms. Non-refundable bookings cannot be treated as refundable unless the supplier approves an exception.

Visa and advisory services

Service or professional fees may cover work already performed and may not be refundable once processing has begun. Government, embassy, biometric, courier and other third-party fees are governed by the relevant provider and may be non-refundable.

Study abroad

Institution deposits, application fees and tuition refunds are governed by the relevant institution. Journey Expert does not control an institution's refund decision or timing.

How to request

Contact Journey Expert with your booking/application reference and the reason for the request. We will confirm the applicable supplier rules and any refundable amount before processing.
